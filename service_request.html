3. Legal Terms, Conditions & Liability Disclaimer

3.1 Mandatory Inspection Fee: A mandatory, non-refundable inspection fee of €50.00 + VAT applies to all device admissions. This fee covers the technical diagnostic resources and bench time required to assess the hardware and is strictly non-refundable under all circumstances, regardless of whether a repair is viable or declined by the customer.

3.2 Upfront Payment Policy: To protect our commercial retail interests, all additional hardware components, replacement parts, and repair service fees must be paid in full upfront before any technical work or parts procurement commences.

3.3 Quotation Validity & Withdrawal (E&OE): All cost estimates and formal price quotes are strictly valid for one (1) working day from the time of issue. Management reserves the absolute right to withdraw from, amend, or cancel a quotation at any time due to shifting hardware stock availability, market supply fluctuations, or unforeseen clerical and pricing errors (Errors and Omissions Excepted / E&OE).

3.4 Data Liability & Backup Release: ITEIREANN strongly advises customers to back up all data prior to handing over any device. While we take every reasonable precaution, ITEIREANN accepts absolutely no liability for the loss, corruption, or compromise of data, software, or configurations during diagnostic or repair processes. Data recovery is never guaranteed.

3.5 Unseen & Pre-existing Faults: Electronic components can fail unpredictably under diagnostic stress or during disassembly, especially if subjected to prior liquid or physical impact. ITEIREANN is not liable for devices that become completely non-operational or exhibit new faults during the course of assessment or repair due to latent, pre-existing structural or electronic degradation.

3.6 Abandoned Property & Disposal Rights: Devices not collected within sixty (60) days of notification of readiness (or completion of diagnosis) will be deemed abandoned. In accordance with legal principles regarding uncollected goods, ITEIREANN reserves the right to dispose of, recycle, or sell the device to recover outstanding diagnostic, repair, storage, or admin fees.
